1||General||Clinic Hours & Holidays||The clinic operates Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. We are closed on weekends and all BC statutory holidays. Phones are unmonitored during lunch hour from 12:00 PM to 1:00 PM.
2||Patient||Doctor's Note / Sick Note||To get a doctor's note for work or school, you must schedule an appointment on the day of your illness. Retroactive sick notes cannot be issued. Please note that a $30 fee applies as this is not covered by MSP.
3||Patient||Lab Results Inquiry||The clinic will only call you if your lab results or imaging reports show abnormal findings requiring follow-up. If the results are normal, you will not receive a call. You can also check them online via MyCareCompass.
4||Patient||Prescription Refills via Fax||We do not accept over-the-phone prescription refills. Please ask your pharmacy to fax a refill request directly to our clinic, or book a brief telehealth appointment with the doctor.
5||Patient||Chasing Specialist Referrals||If you are calling to check the status of a specialist referral, please note that triage times are determined entirely by the specialist's office, usually taking 2 to 4 weeks. We will notify you as soon as we receive their appointment date.
6||Patient||Running Late Policy||If you are running more than 10 minutes late for your scheduled appointment, you may be asked to resubmit your request for a same-day urgent slot, or reschedule to another day to avoid disrupting other patients.
7||Patient||Telehealth vs In-Person||Virtual phone appointments are strictly for reviewing stable chronic conditions or test results. Any new symptoms, physical pain, prescriptions for controlled substances, or infections require an in-person physical exam.
8||Urgent||Emergency Protocol||[CRITICAL] If you are experiencing a medical emergency, such as severe chest pain, shortness of breath, sudden weakness, or heavy bleeding, please hang up immediately and dial 911 or proceed to the nearest Emergency Room.
9||General||New Patients Waitlist||We are currently NOT accepting new patients. You can register on the BC Health Connect Registry to be placed on a centralized waitlist for a family doctor.
10||Patient||Cancellation & No-Show Policy||You must provide at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment. Failure to do so, or missing an appointment (No-Show), will result in a $40-$80 fee that must be paid before your next visit.
11||Patient||Uninsured Services Fees||Services like Driver's Medical Forms, insurance paperwork, and travel immunizations are not covered by MSP. Patients are responsible for these private fees, payable by cash/debit before forms are completed.
12||Patient||Out-of-Province / Non-Residents||Patients without a valid BC MSP card (including tourists and out-of-province residents) will be charged a private consultation fee ($100-$150) at the time of check-in. A receipt will be issued for your private insurance.
13||Patient||Walk-in Appointments||Yes, we do accept walk-in patients. Walk-in slots open at 9:00 AM daily. Due to high demand, we may stop accepting walk-ins early if we reach maximum capacity. We recommend calling ahead to check availability, especially later in the day.
14||Patient||How to Book an Appointment||Registered patients can book an appointment through our online portal (MyHealth/Medeo) 24/7, which is the fastest method. Alternatively, you can call our reception desk during clinic hours. Please have your Personal Health Number (PHN) ready.
15||Patient||Same-Day Urgent Appointments||For acute, non-life-threatening illnesses (e.g., sudden infections, high fever), we release a limited number of Same-Day Urgent slots every morning at 9:00 AM. These are allocated on a first-come, first-served basis via phone or online.
16||Patient||Booking Appointments for Dependents||When booking for family members (children or elderly parents), please ensure the appointment is created under their individual name and CareCard (PHN) number, not the primary account holder's name.
17||General||Clinic Location||Our clinic is located in Road 3,Richmond, BC. 
18||General||Clinic Website & Portal||You can visit our official website at www.harvey-clinic.com to access the patient portal, book appointments online, or download intake forms.
19||General||Clinic Parking||Parking is available on-site for patients, with designated stalls near the main entrance.
